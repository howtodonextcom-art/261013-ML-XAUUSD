# Scalping candidates C2 and C3: pre-registration (Level 6.5)

Written and committed BEFORE any C2 code exists. Nothing here has been run. No price outcome of any kind has been looked at for these candidates (the audit of docs/research/SCALPING_OPPORTUNITY_AUDIT.md contains counts and classifications only). V1 (`cand-1`), V2 (`m1v2-1`), P6 and P7 are untouched and unaffected: C2 is a separate strategy with its own id, version, state machine, events and log; it never changes a V1 or V2 decision, never enters the Risk Gate, and is SHADOW only (READ_ONLY + SHADOW + PAPER_SIMULATION).

## Options considered
| | Idea | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| C0 | frozen control: V1 | unchanged, the reference |
| C1 | existing shadow V2 (M15 zone + M5 confirmation + M1 trigger, H1/H4 hard gate) | unchanged, a benchmark (B1) |
| **C2** | M5 structure gives the direction; M15 gives zone and volatility; M1 gives the trigger and timing; H1/H4 is a LABEL (ALIGNED / COUNTERTREND / NEUTRAL / UNKNOWN), never a gate | **BUILD as a forward-only shadow** |
| C3 | momentum / breakout continuation without the V1 pullback (the `MISSED` and `SUPERSEDED_BY_CONTINUATION` cases) | **NOT BUILT: proposal only** (section C3) |
Why C2 and not C3 first: C2 answers the audited gap directly (V1 and V2 are blind to the 49.7% no-H1-direction and 18.1% countertrend M5 structure time) with the least new machinery (V2's frozen trigger and cost model, one changed layer). C3 needs a breakout definition whose entries an M5 bar cannot evaluate honestly (no intrabar order, no spread) and M1 development data does not exist; building both would dilute the sample and the review.

## C2 hypothesis and benchmarks
**Hypothesis (H-C2):** M1 closed-bar triggers at an M15 zone, taken in the direction of the M5 structure and not gated by H1/H4, have positive net expectancy after costs, and the countertrend subset is not worse than the aligned subset. **Null:** no better than random M1 entries with the same stop/target geometry. Benchmarks on the same forward period: B0 no trade; B1 V2 trades (`scalping` coverage and expectancy compared); B2 random M1 entries, 500 seeds, same stop/target distances and the same costs; B3 the two subsets (ALIGNED vs COUNTERTREND) of C2 itself.

## C2 rules (all causal; every event carries `known_at` = the close time of the bar that made it knowable; closed bars only; the forming M1/M5 bar is display-only and never an event)
- **Strategy id** `scalp-c2`, version `scalp-c2-1`, own state machine, own events, own log `data/scalp/c2_events.jsonl`.
- **Direction (M5 structure):** `structure_trend_series` on closed M5 bars (zig-zag k = 2.5, swings known at their `known_idx`): UP -> BUY side only, DOWN -> SELL side only, RANGE / UNKNOWN -> no side (WAIT). BUY and SELL are evaluated independently, no shared state, exact price mirror.
- **M15 zone:** the nearest zone-1 zone (confirmed swings only) on the correct side: BUY needs `zone.high <= price + 0.25 ATR15` and `zone.high >= price - 2.0 ATR15` (SELL mirrored). M15 ATR is recorded as volatility context (no filter).
- **H1/H4 label (not a gate):** H1 direction aligned to the closed H1 bar: ALIGNED (same as the side), COUNTERTREND (opposite), NEUTRAL (H1 NONE), UNKNOWN; H4 conflict flag recorded. The label is stored in every event.
- **M1 trigger (closed M1 bars only, price inside the band `[zone.low - 0.25 ATR5, zone.high + 0.5 ATR5]`)**, any ONE of: rejection (pin), engulfing, momentum shift (close above the highest high of the previous 3 bars, range >= 0.8 ATR(M1), after at least 2 lower closes in the previous 4), structure break (first close above the last confirmed M1 swing high, k = 2.5). Definitions are exactly V2's (`pa-1`, `entry_v2`); SELL mirrored.
- **States per side:** NO_SIDE, NO_ZONE, WATCH (side + zone, no trigger), TRIGGERED, ENTRY_WINDOW (up to 5 M1 bars after the trigger close, close within 0.25 ATR5 of the trigger close), then terminal MISSED (price ran away), INVALIDATED (a closed M1 bar beyond `zone.low - 0.25 ATR5`, or the M5 structure flips to the opposite side), EXPIRED (window ended). A trigger needs the M5 side to hold at the trigger bar's close.
- **Expiry:** a watch has no expiry of its own (it is a state, recomputed every closed bar); a trigger is actionable for the entry window only (5 M1 minutes), then terminal.
- **Entry / exits / costs (identical to the registered V2 model, so the frozen evaluator is reused):** fill at the OPEN of the M1 bar after the trigger bar (the bar stamped trigger + 60 s must exist), ask-aware for BUY / bid for SELL; spread = the last closed M1 bar's spread with fixed-spread floors 0.2 and 0.4 USD as mandatory sensitivity; slippage 0 / 0.1 / 0.3 USD; commission as in `COSTS`; SL = `min(trigger low, zone.low) - 0.25 ATR5` (SELL mirrored); **TP = 2.0 x the stop distance** (no dependence on V1); timeout 288 M1 minutes of timeline; one open hypothetical position per direction.
- **Risk and broker constraints (not negotiable):** the same `risk-1` plan checks and `gate-1` on the REFERENCE account: the stop distance must be at least the broker stops level (6.00 USD on MetaQuotes-Demo XAUUSD), net R:R at least 1.5 after costs, the spread / ATR limit. A setup that does not fit is **BLOCKED** with the failing code: the stop is never widened to pass. Margin and minimum lot are those of the reference-account plan.
- **No trade:** stale data, H4/H1 label UNKNOWN is allowed (recorded), missing entry bar, broker spread spike (gate-1), any BLOCKED, a position already open in that direction.

## Dataset isolation (decided from the facts, before any outcome)
All stored M1 history (from 2026-08-04) lies inside the sealed P6/P7 holdout period: **C2 gets no historical performance claim.** Allowed on historical M1: synthetic correctness tests and a counts-only signal-frequency funnel (no price outcome). C2 evidence comes only from events produced live AFTER the commit of this document (`data/scalp/c2_events.jsonl`, append-only, git-ignored, market data and decision-time evidence only, no account figures). Evaluation is refused before BOTH at least 100 TRIGGERED events AND at least 30 calendar days after the registration commit (a pure UTC difference); before that the status is INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.

## Evaluation (frozen now; reuses the frozen V2 evaluator, amendments A1-A15 included)
Net expectancy per trade in R with a 95% day-block bootstrap interval (2000 resamples, seed 20261014), win rate, profit factor, max drawdown (R), by side / trigger type / alignment class / hour; cost sensitivity; funnel (watch -> triggered -> risk-passed -> filled -> outcome); opportunity coverage versus V1 and V2 (share of C2 triggers in which V1 / V2 had a setup on the same side); delivery latency (closed-bar confirmation to publication); drawdown, tail risk, trade frequency (overtrading risk). Walk-forward = chronological quarters of the forward period. No ML, so no calibration claim. **C2 shows evidence only if all hold:** >= 100 triggered and filled trades; mean net R > 0 at base cost, at +0.3 slippage and at the 0.4 spread floor; bootstrap lower bound > 0; above the 95th percentile of B2; positive in at least 3 of 4 chronological quarters; no single week above 40% of total R. The subset comparison B3 is informational (it cannot rescue or sink the verdict; a verdict is never computed on a subset chosen after looking). Otherwise INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE or NO_EVIDENCE_OF_EDGE. **Rejection:** NO_EVIDENCE_OF_EDGE at the first evaluation, or a causality / mirror / duplicate-event defect found by review that cannot be fixed without changing a registered rule. **Acceptance for anything beyond SHADOW:** a separate new registration; C2 stays SHADOW whatever the trigger count.
No threshold above may be changed after the first forward outcome is looked at; a change is a new version (`scalp-c2-2`) with its own registration and clock.

## Guards
Prefix invariance and future-corruption tests (synthetic, mutation-checked), exact BUY/SELL mirror, determinism (same bars -> identical events), forming bars never enter an event, duplicate-id protection, restart/reconnect dedup (ids persisted before use), V1 `decision` and the V2 section byte-identical with and without C2 (tested), C2 failure or slowness cannot delay V1 or V2 publication (own try/except, own job deadline), no LLM and no synchronous model call anywhere on the tick path.

## C3 (momentum / breakout): proposal only, not built, not run
Question: do continuation breakouts (a closed M5 bar beyond the last confirmed M5 swing with range >= 1.0 ATR(M5), M15 volatility not contracting, in either direction with the H1 label recorded) have positive net expectancy after costs on data not used to define them? Why not now: the missed-opportunity classes of V1 (`MISSED`: 15 per side, `SUPERSEDED_BY_CONTINUATION`: 28 BUY in dev+val) show that the moves exist, but they are moves that happened; whether a causal rule would have caught them with tradable entry and a 6.00 USD stop is unknown. Before any code: its own registration with exact breakout and invalidation rules, entry timing on an M1 bar, costs, minimum sample, and a dataset plan (M5 development data exists up to `val_end`; it can be used ONCE for a single pre-registered validation run; M1 execution realism is forward-only). C3 is not part of C2 and is not implemented by this document.

## Limits known in advance
The 100-trigger minimum may take many weeks; C2 is expected to fire more often than V2 (no H1/H4 gate, no M5 pattern requirement), which raises overtrading risk and correlation between triggers (several triggers in one zone: one trade per direction at a time); one instrument and feed; spread data quality; the M1 history limit of the terminal; forward results describe the future period only; a higher trigger count is not evidence of a higher expectancy.
