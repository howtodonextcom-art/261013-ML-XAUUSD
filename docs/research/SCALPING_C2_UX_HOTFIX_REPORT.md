# Level 6.5.1 hotfix report: scalping UX and signal semantics (RESEARCH BETA, SHADOW, PAPER)

No V1, V2 or C2 rule, threshold or registered model changed. No order function exists on any path. The sealed P6/P7 holdout was not opened.

1. **Signal semantics (P0).** Backend and UI now distinguish: WATCH BUY/SELL < M1 TRIGGER (closed-bar research trigger, NOT through the Risk Gate) < ENTRY WINDOW < **C2 BUY/SELL PAPER** (only a LIVE_FORWARD trigger in ENTRY_WINDOW that passed the Risk Gate) / **C2 BLOCKED** / HISTORICAL (RE_DERIVED at startup: grey "C2 hist", never current, never a signal or plan). A trigger marker reads "C2 TRIG", never "C2 BUY". Provenance fails safe: unknown or stale UTC offset gives RE_DERIVED. Amendment A10.
2. **Scalping-first UI (P1).** The first right-hand card shows Market bias | M1 momentum | M5 structure | C2 candidate | C2 research signal | Missing condition | Risk status, large text, colour + arrow icons, RESEARCH BETA badge. V1 Decision is a collapsible reference panel (state remembered); V2 shadow unchanged. Chart not rebuilt.
3. **Entry/risk consistency (P2).** Not a bug: the registered evaluator (`evaluate_v2.plan_for`) also builds levels from the trigger close, and the registered fill is the next M1 open. The card now says so (`entry_basis`) and shows `expected_fill` and its delta against the plan entry once that open exists. No model change.
4. **Performance (P3).** A/B on the live feed, ~6 to 7 minutes each: gateway tick-to-publication p50/p95/p99 = 1.27/2.82/4.17 ms (C2 off, n=2310) vs 1.23/2.55/4.03 ms (C2 on, n=2934), no slowdown. C2 compute p50 175 ms, p95 339 ms (n=8, small sample, one steady-state run); V2 184/349 ms; V1 heavy 255/510 ms. Eligibility-to-publication p50 2.1 s, p95 3.1 s (n=7). Browser render of the C2 card p50 ~0 ms, p99 0.2 ms (n=17). Samples are SMALL; timeouts and skipped bars are now counted. p99 is not meaningful at n<100.
5. **Tests.** 530 pytest pass. Playwright headless: 121 passed, 5 skipped (visual baselines are headed-only). Headed: smoke, visual, scalp, inventory 17/17. The 3 earlier headless failures were investigated: the smoke check asserted "not HeadlessChrome" (always false headless) and 2 visual baselines differ by headless anti-aliasing; both are now guarded to run headed only, and the baselines were regenerated for the new card header.
6. **Chrome MCP (real app).** M1/M5/M15/H1/H4 switching, layer toggle (3 hist markers on, 0 off), V1 collapse/expand, stale clear and recovery, 1440x900 and a narrow viewport. A live "ENTRY WINDOW BUY / C2 BUY PAPER" with LIVE FORWARD provenance and Risk Gate PASSED (stop 9.82 USD, net R:R 1.78) was observed once on the real feed; this is a paper research observation, not an order. Reconnect was not exercised separately (WebSocket drop not simulated in this pass).
7. **Independent review.** Found 5 narrower defects (thread-unsafe stats deque, elapsed-window label, incomplete-trigger label, latency survivorship, C2 launch coupled to the V2 job); all fixed with tests. No path found that shows a trigger or re-derived event as a signal.
8. **Limitations.** Small latency samples; reconnect not separately simulated; a live trigger can show both a "C2 TRIG" and a "C2 BUY PAPER" marker on one bar; evidence still needs >= 100 triggers and 30 days; a paper signal is not a profit claim.
