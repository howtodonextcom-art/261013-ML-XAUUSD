Generated by `benchmarks/latency_report.py` from `results/p3/*.json`. Conditions, sample sizes and the clock caveats are part of every figure; nothing is extrapolated and nothing was improved by dropping ticks or skipping risk checks.

Market leg (gateway receipt minus the tick's server stamp, converted with the measured offset): p50 -322 ms, i.e. NEGATIVE: the broker's tick stamp is ahead of this PC's clock by roughly 0.3 s or more. The two clocks are not synchronised, so the broker-to-PC latency cannot be derived from these timestamps and none is claimed.
A hidden tab renders late by design (the browser pauses animation frames): background_tab render p50/p95 above. The 100-drawings scenario is measured after a 10 s settle (the first seconds after a reload are slower: history fetch plus drawings). CPU load: 8 busy processes on 16 logical cores.

## Reading this honestly

- The earlier 29 ms gateway p99 after a restart is explained by the in-process heavy recompute (scheduling delay p99 16-20 ms, max 27 ms here); with the worker process the same loop shows p99 about 0.1 ms. Production now uses the worker process.
- Live gateway receipt-to-publish after the change: p50 about 1.1 ms, p99 about 6.8 ms (n=2033, one M15 bar close observed with max 10.2 ms); a systematic series across many bar closes has NOT been collected.
- The figures describe this machine, this Chrome and this load; they are not a guarantee. There is no zero-latency claim.

Render p99 is unchanged (16 versus 16-17 ms, inside the +20% gate registered before the change). The A/B run with the OLD UI files shows the same gateway and delivery figures as the new UI, so the shift of the tick-to-browser time (about 2.4 ms to about 6.3 ms p50, the sum of the two p99 values, which is not a measured end-to-end percentile, about 8.7 to 13.7 ms) is NOT caused by the redesign. It appeared between the morning baseline and the later runs (backend changes of Levels 6.2 and 6.3, or machine/browser state); the cause was not isolated. It stays under 15 ms p99. The market-side delay (broker to PC) is still not measurable (clock skew).
